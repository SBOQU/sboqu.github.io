    // Stages

    Two Stages, One Housing

    Same 11 rollers, same 10-lobe pattern. Stage 1 runs fast and light, stage 2 slow and loaded.

    Stage 1 · High Speed

    4 000 → 400 rpm · 25 N·m
    Eccentricity
    3,0 mm
    Lobes / rollers
    10 / 11 on Ø118
    Eccentric bearing
    INA HK 1210
    Output pins
    10 × Ø6 on Ø52
    Rollers per pin
    1 × SKF 61903
    Roller life
    2 078 830 h
    Roller contact 364 MPa ✓ Limit 2 000 MPa
    Pin contact 343 MPa ✓ Limit 1 800 MPa
    Oil film λ 6,0 ✓ Full film, above 3
    Eccentric bearing life 1 438 h ✓ Target 500 h

    Every contact stays under a fifth of its limit, on a full oil film. The needle bearing sets the life: 710 h once the unbalance of the disc is counted, still above the target.

    Stage 2 · High Torque

    400 → 40 rpm · 250 N·m
    Eccentricity
    3,5 mm
    Lobes / rollers
    10 / 11 on Ø118
    Eccentric bearing
    SKF NJ 204 ECP
    Output pins
    12 × Ø4 on Ø64
    Rollers per pin
    2 × SKF 61903
    Roller load
    847 N, 1 695 N peak
    Roller contact 990 MPa ✓ Limit 2 000 MPa
    Pin contact 1 043 MPa ✓ Limit 1 800 MPa
    Oil film λ 0,8 ! Target above 1
    Eccentric bearing life 3 816 h ✓ Target 500 h

    Ten times the torque at a tenth of the speed: the pressures keep their margin, but the film on the pins is thinner than the roughness. A finer grinding, Rq 0,1 µm, doubles λ to 1,6.
